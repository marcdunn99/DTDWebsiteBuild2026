Psychic Readings

A gentle, intuitive session focused entirely on you, your emotions, experiences, and the path you are currently walking.

By tuning into your soul's awareness, insight may arise around past influences that still hold meaning, current situations or patterns, and the choices available to you now. Your natural strengths, abilities, and deeper potential often come forward, including qualities that may be ready to be recognised or developed.

I offer insight and clarity into many areas of your life, including relationships, love, career path, and family, helping you navigate life's challenges with confidence and understanding.

While a reading can reveal opportunities, guidance, and potential pathways, we all have free will. It is the choices you make that shape your life and future.

Mediumship Readings

A compassionate and heartfelt opportunity to connect with loved ones in the spirit world, bringing comfort, reassurance, and healing.

As a medium, I work as a link between you and spirit, allowing your loved ones to come forward in their own unique way. This may be through personality traits, memories, shared experiences, or specific details that help you recognise them.

Messages often carry love, reassurance, and gentle guidance, reminding you that your loved ones remain close, aware, and supportive of your life today.

For many, a mediumship reading can ease grief, soften unanswered questions, and bring a sense of peace. Clients often leave feeling calmer, lighter, and reassured that love continues beyond physical death.

Events & Demonstrations

A mediumship demonstration is a public or private event where a medium connects with the spirit world to deliver messages to members of the audience. These sessions aim to provide evidence of life after death through specific and identifying details and may be held in theatres, churches, centres, private venues, or via online platforms.

Whilst not everyone receives a personal reading, the experience is intended to be uplifting and healing for all.

A good mediumistic experience is one where the client or recipient clearly recognises who has come through and can feel the loved one's presence during the reading.

About Denise

I have been deeply sensitive to the emotions and energies of others from a young age, with an intuitive awareness that developed naturally over time. Alongside this was a quiet inner knowing of life beyond physical death, long before I undertook any formal spiritual training.

My abilities are part of a family lineage, with both my maternal grandmother and a cousin working as mediums. However, it was the passing of my mother to the spirit world at the age of 47 that marked a profound turning point in my spiritual journey. Seeking understanding and connection, I began attending a Spiritualist Church, where my own mediumistic abilities were gently awakened and developed.

I trained formally within the Spiritualist Church, working in a grounded, ethical, and evidence-based way. Alongside this, I trained in Reiki and later became a Reiki Master, which deepened my sensitivity and understanding of healing on all levels.

I have completed extensive training, including study at the Arthur Findlay College, UK, and hold certificates from the Spiritualists' National Union (SNU) in Demonstrating Mediumship and Public Speaking (CSNU). I currently work as a demonstrating medium in Spiritualist Churches and Centres, teach mediumship, and offer private one-to-one readings for clients worldwide.

Alongside my spiritual work, my professional background as a District Nurse — including years within palliative care — has shaped my approach profoundly. This experience gives me a deep understanding of grief, loss, and human vulnerability, allowing me to work with compassion for the whole person: mind, body, and spirit.
